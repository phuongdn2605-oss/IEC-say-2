01 — Introduction

Who we are

Established on June 13th, 2023, the RMIT Innovation and Entrepreneurship Club Hanoi fosters a vibrant and inspirational environment for young spirits with a heart for innovation and entrepreneurship.

We have made significant strides in promoting innovation and entrepreneurship within the RMIT and Hanoi student communities through impactful and diverse events. Our destined path is to empower RMIT students to become innovative leaders, equipped with the tools, knowledge, and opportunities to realize their full potential, transform their business ideas into reality, and to create positive change.

Ambition

Achieving at least 1 unicorn startup founded by students coming from Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club.

Vision

To empower RMIT students to become innovative and entrepreneurial leaders, inspiring them to create positive change in their communities and beyond.

Mission

To provide real start-up experience to RMIT students

  • ›fostering their entrepreneurial spirit
  • ›cultivating a vibrant community of innovators dedicated to driving positive societal impact.

Events

Sem 1 · 2025Interactive Exhibition & Talkshow

From Chaos to Crown

“From Chaos to Crown” is a culmination of our most successful past events, thoughtfully woven into a one-day experience that blends engagement, insight, and creativity. The morning features an interactive showcase complete with hands-on crafting workshops and dynamic minigames, fostering collaboration and exploration. In the afternoon, we shift gears with a thought-provoking talk show and pitch session.

Through the academic and real-world insights of our guest speakers, we explore the Three Stages of Communication Crisis model: pre-crisis, crisis response, and post-crisis recovery — brought to life through personal stories, professional experiences, and actionable strategies.

Sem 2 · 2025LinkedIn Workshop

LinkedIn – LockedIn

“LinkedIn, LockedIn” was a dynamic professional development event designed to empower students to elevate their LinkedIn profiles and networking confidence through a hands-on, one-day experience. The morning began with an insightful talk show, where guest speakers Mr. Cuong Dang and Ms. Hang Pham shared practical strategies on personal branding, professional outreach, and building meaningful connections on LinkedIn.

In the afternoon, participants engaged in an interactive workshop, applying what they learned to refine their own profiles — from crafting compelling headlines to articulating impactful experience descriptions.

By combining expert insights, practical learning, and creative engagement, LinkedIn, LockedIn embodied the mission of the RMIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Club: to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world professional growth.

Sem 3 · 2025Interactive Gameplay

Innoventure

Startup ideas aren’t rare. What’s rare is knowing how to move forward when nothing feels certain yet.

When you’re building something new, clarity doesn’t arrive first. You move with limited time, limited resources, and incomplete information. Every decision opens one path and quietly closes another. Sooner or later, every team stands at the same crossroads:

  • ›PLAY SAFE: Move carefully. Reduce risk. Stay within what feels familiar.
  • ›ALL-IN: Commit early. Push fast. Accept that mistakes will cost you.

The question remains: ALL-IN or PLAY SAFE? Discover your entrepreneurial style at Innoventure!

Sem 1 · 2026Charity Event

Joyful Delivery

The “Joyful Delivery” initiative by IEC is a meaningful community project designed to spread happiness and connect deeply with the children at the Sao Mai Center. Built on the core belief that charity does not have to be something heavy and can simply start with a smile, the event focuses on transforming small, genuine moments into impactful experiences. By engaging participants in a shared journey of giving, the project bridges the gap between community action and heartfelt compassion.

Create

In the initial “CREATE” stage, participants visit interactive workshops featuring minigames to generate positive energy. This phase raises essential funds while allowing players to empathize with the children and write warm, handwritten notes.

Deliver

The initiative culminates in the “DELIVER” stage, where IEC brings the raised funds and positive energy directly to the Sao Mai Center. During a recent visit, the team distributed gifts and spent quality time drawing, talking, and playing with the kids. These simple activities quickly blossomed into deep, real connections through shared laughter and colors.

Sem 1 · 2026Interactive Exhibition

Giao — IEC × Trang Bùi

The “Giao” Exhibit is an interactive, multi-phase exhibition co-created through a strategic partnership between the RMIT Innovation & Entrepreneurship Hanoi (IEC) and the Vietnamese-inspired design brand TRANG BÙI. Modeled around the formal Entrepreneurial Process Model, this collaboration aims to translate a real-world fashion case study into a structured academic journey, guiding visitors step-by-step through the lifecycle of a modern startup.

Ultimately, “Giao” — signifying the intersection of tradition and modernity — delivers the message that cultural heritage is a dynamic catalyst for innovation rather than a relic of the past. By transforming restrictive traditional attire into functional, unisex everyday wear, the exhibit proves that honoring one’s roots is fully compatible with contemporary utility, inspiring future entrepreneurs to leverage cultural identity as a powerful foundation for sustainable business.
